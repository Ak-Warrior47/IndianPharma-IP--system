📘 Admin Guide — How to Manage Staff
➕
Adding a New Staff Member
  1. Scroll down to "Add New Staff Member" below
  2. Type the employee's Full Name (auto-capitalised)
  3. Enter their Email — type name, select @pharmaip.com
  4. Choose a Password (minimum 6 characters)
  5. Select their role: Picker or Checker
  6. Click "+ Add Member" — done!
✅ Staff can log in immediately after being added
🔑
Resetting a Password
  1. Find the staff member in the table below
  2. Click the 🔑 key icon in the Actions column
  3. A popup will appear — type the new password
  4. Password must be at least 6 characters
  5. Click "Reset Password"
  6. Staff can use the new password immediately
⚠️ Always share the new password securely with the staff member
🗑️
Removing a Staff Member
  1. Find the employee in the Staff Performance table
  2. Click the 🗑️ trash icon in their row
  3. A confirmation box will appear — click OK to confirm
  4. The account and all their KPI data will be removed
❗ Deletion is permanent. All past data will be lost.

📐 KPI Formula Reference
PICKER
Pick Accuracy = Picked ÷ (Picked+Missed) × 100
Pick Speed = (Picked+Missed) ÷ Total Time (hrs)
Packing Eff. = Packing Done ÷ Sales Bill × 100
CS Fulfilment = min(Packing Done ÷ CS Open × 100, 100)
Efficiency Score = (Acc/100)×50 + min(Spd/200,1)×30 + (PkgEff/100)×12 + (CS/100)×8
CHECKER
Clean Check Rate = (Checked−Errors) ÷ Checked × 100
Error Rate = Errors ÷ Checked × 100
Check Speed = Checked ÷ Check Time (hrs)
Efficiency Score = (CleanRate/100)×70 + min(CkSpd/150,1)×30
GRADES: ELITE ≥98% | PROFICIENT ≥95% | SATISFACTORY ≥88% | RE-TRAINING <88%
