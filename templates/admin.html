KRA System · Formulas & Instructions

Picker KPIs & Formulas

Sales Bills OpenSales bills packed incl. urgent bills for the day
Item PickedItems successfully picked from rack
Item MissedItems not found or incorrectly picked
CS Sales OpenCS open orders handled
Packing DoneItems packed and ready for dispatch
Rack OrganizedYes/No — rack organized at end of shift
Table CleanYes/No — workstation table cleaned
Total Time (mins)Total shift working time in minutes

Pick Accuracy = Picked ÷ (Picked+Missed) × 100

Pick Speed = (Picked+Missed) ÷ Total Time (hrs)

Packing Eff. (display) = Packing Done ÷ Sales Bills Packed × 100

Pack Rate (in score) = Packing Done ÷ (Picked+Missed) × 100

CS Fulfilment = min(Packing Done ÷ CS Sales Open × 100, 100)

Workspace Score = (Rack+Table Days) ÷ (2×Days) × 100

Efficiency Score = (Acc/100)×55 + min(Speed/200,1)×30 + (Workspace/100)×10 + (PackRate/100)×5

ELITE ≥98%+Workspace≥80% · PROFICIENT ≥95% · SATISFACTORY ≥88% · RE-TRAINING <88%

Checker KPIs & Formulas

Sales Bills OpenBills verified during checking shift
Item Picked/MissedItems picked/missed during checking assist
CS Sales OpenCS orders verified
Packing DonePacking verifications completed
Rack OrganizedYes/No — rack organized after checking
Table CleanYes/No — checking table cleaned
Items CheckedTotal items verified
Errors FoundItems with picking/packing errors detected
Check Time (mins)Time spent on checking in minutes

Check Accuracy = (Checked−Errors) ÷ Checked × 100

Error Rate = Errors ÷ Checked × 100

Check Speed = Checked ÷ Check Time (hrs)

Workspace Score = (Rack+Table Days) ÷ (2×Days) × 100

Efficiency Score = (CheckAcc/100)×65 + min(Speed/150,1)×25 + (Workspace/100)×10

ELITE ≥97%+Workspace≥80% · PROFICIENT ≥94% · SATISFACTORY ≥87% · RE-TRAINING <87%
