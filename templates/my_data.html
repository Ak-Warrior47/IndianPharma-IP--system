Location tracking
The most sensitive thing recorded here, so it is spelled out in full.
  • Only during a delivery trip. Your location is recorded after you press Start Trip and stops the moment the trip is finished. Nothing is recorded before, after, or on a day you are not driving.
  • Roughly every {{ gps_min_seconds }} seconds, and only if you have moved {{ gps_min_metres }} metres. Points closer together than that are discarded rather than stored, so the record shows the route you drove and not your every movement.
  • Deleted automatically after {{ gps_retention_days }} days. Location points are erased on a schedule. The delivery record itself is kept, but the detailed trail is not.
  • Seen only by you and the admin. No colleague can see where you have been, and nothing is shared outside this system.
  • Your phone decides. If you refuse the browser's location prompt, the trip still works. Delivery is recorded without the route.

What is not held
  • Your password. Only a one-way hash is stored. Nobody, including the admin, can read it back. A reset sends you a code rather than revealing anything.
  • Location outside a trip. See above.
  • Anything shared with third parties. Your records are not sold, and are not sent anywhere outside this system.
