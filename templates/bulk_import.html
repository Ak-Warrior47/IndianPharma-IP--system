name,email,password,staff_type

Validation rules:
  • All columns required: name, email, password, staff_type
  • Password must be at least 8 characters
  • staff_type must be one of: picker, checker, purchaser, delivery, biller, packer, supervisor
  • Email must be unique (not already in system)
  • Rows that fail validation are skipped with an explanation
