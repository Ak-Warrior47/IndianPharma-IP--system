📐 Formula Reference — How scores are calculated
{% if emp.staff_type == 'picker' %}
Pick Accuracy = Items Picked ÷ (Picked + Missed) × 100
Pick Speed = (Picked + Missed) ÷ (9 hrs × Days)
Packing Eff. = Packing Done ÷ Sales Bill Picked × 100
CS Fulfilment = min(Packing Done ÷ CS Sales Open × 100, 100)
Workspace Score = (Rack + Table Days) ÷ (2 × Days) × 100
Efficiency Score = Acc×55 + Speed×30 + Workspace×10 + Packing×5
Potential Items = 200 × 9 hrs × Days
Consistency = max(0, 100 − std_dev(daily_accuracy) × 2)
ELITE: Acc≥98% + Workspace≥80%
PROFICIENT: Acc≥95% · SATISFACTORY: Acc≥88%
{% else %}
Total SB Checked = SB Normal + SB Urgent (urgent done first)
Total Items Checked = Items Normal + Urgent Items (both = good work)
Normal % = Items Normal ÷ Total Items × 100 (info only)
Urgent % = Urgent Items ÷ Total Items × 100 (info only)
Check Speed = Total Items Checked ÷ (9 hrs × Days)
Efficiency Score = min(Check Speed ÷ 25, 1) × 100
Potential Items = 25 items/hr × 9 hrs × Days
Consistency = max(0, 100 − std_dev(daily_speed) × 2)
ELITE: Speed ≥28/hr · PROFICIENT: ≥22/hr
SATISFACTORY: ≥15/hr · RE-TRAINING: <15/hr
